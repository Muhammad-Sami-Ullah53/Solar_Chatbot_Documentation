## Source Priority

1. Current Pakistan law/regulation or NEPRA notification
2. Current official utility notification
3. Current manufacturer datasheet/manual
4. Reputable industry source
5. Project research document
6. General educational source

## RAG Rules

* Keep content concise and question-answer friendly.
* Avoid long copy-pasted passages.
* Date-stamp changing information.
* Do not treat old prices or regulations as permanent facts.
* Prefer official manufacturer specifications.
* Prefer official government/regulatory sources for Pakistan policy.
