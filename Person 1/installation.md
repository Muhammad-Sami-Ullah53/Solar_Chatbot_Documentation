# Solar Installation, Maintenance and Warranty

## Customer Assessment
Collect location, monthly consumption, bills, major appliances, peak/critical load, backup requirement, roof area, grid availability, system preference and budget.

## Site Survey
Inspect roof condition, dimensions, structure, shading, orientation, cable routes, distribution board, inverter/battery locations, earthing and safe access.

## Installation Process
1. Site survey
2. Load assessment
3. System design
4. Mounting structure
5. Panel installation
6. DC wiring
7. Inverter installation
8. Battery installation if required
9. AC connection and protection
10. Earthing/grounding
11. Testing
12. Commissioning

## Protection
Depending on the design, use appropriate DC/AC isolators, breakers/fuses, surge protection, earthing, correctly sized cables and battery protection.

## Testing
Verify polarity, string voltage/current, insulation, earthing, inverter settings, grid parameters, battery communication, protection and monitoring.

## Maintenance
Inspect panels for dirt, cracks, hot spots, loose mounting and damaged cables. Check inverter alarms, ventilation, monitoring and fault history. Check batteries for state of charge, temperature, alarms, communication and physical condition.

## Cleaning
The project research suggests 6–12 months as general guidance, but dusty locations may require more frequent cleaning. Follow the manufacturer's instructions.

## Warranty
- **Product warranty:** qualifying product defects under manufacturer terms.
- **Performance warranty:** specified output retention over time.
- **Inverter warranty:** coverage under inverter manufacturer terms.
- **Battery warranty:** may depend on years, cycles, throughput, DoD and operating conditions.

Never promise warranty coverage unless the applicable warranty document confirms it.

## Grid Connection
Grid-connected systems may require utility approval, interconnection and metering procedures. Requirements can change and can differ by utility.

## Safety
Solar equipment can remain energized in sunlight. Energized electrical and battery equipment should only be handled by qualified personnel.
