# Solar FAQ

## 1. What is solar energy?
Energy from sunlight. PV systems convert sunlight into electrical energy.

## 2. How do solar panels work?
PV cells convert sunlight into DC electricity; an inverter can convert it into AC.

## 3. What is an inverter?
A device that converts DC electricity to AC and may provide MPPT, monitoring and protection.

## 4. What is MPPT?
Maximum Power Point Tracking; it helps a PV system operate near its maximum available power point.

## 5. What is the difference between kW and kWh?
kW measures power; kWh measures energy.

## 6. What is an on-grid system?
A solar system connected to the utility grid.

## 7. What is an off-grid system?
A system designed to operate independently of the grid, normally with batteries.

## 8. What is a hybrid system?
A system combining solar, battery and grid operation.

## 9. Do I need batteries?
Not always. Batteries are mainly useful for backup, self-consumption and off-grid operation.

## 10. What is battery DoD?
Depth of Discharge: the percentage of nominal battery energy discharged within specified operating limits.

## 11. Lithium vs lead-acid?
Lithium/LFP generally offers higher usable energy and cycle life, while lead-acid can have lower initial cost.

## 12. How many panels do I need?
It depends on consumption, solar resource, efficiency, panel wattage, roof area and electrical design.

## 13. How many 550 W panels make about 5 kW?
5000/550 = 9.09, so at least 10 panels by simple capacity rounding. String and inverter limits must also be checked.

## 14. How much can a 5 kW system generate?
A simplified estimate is `5 × PSH × system efficiency`. Actual output varies with conditions.

## 15. Do panels work on cloudy days?
Yes, but output is generally lower because available sunlight is lower.

## 16. Does shading matter?
Yes. Shading can significantly reduce PV output.

## 17. Do panels need cleaning?
Yes. Dust and debris can reduce output.

## 18. Which direction should panels face in Pakistan?
South is generally a useful starting direction for fixed systems, but the site determines the final design.

## 19. Can solar work during a power outage?
A standard grid-tied system generally shuts down. A properly designed hybrid/off-grid backup system can supply protected loads.

## 20. What is net billing?
Pakistan's current prosumer framework governs how electricity imported by a prosumer and electricity supplied by the prosumer to the licensee are treated. Use the latest NEPRA rules.

## 21. Does solar eliminate the electricity bill?
Not necessarily. Fixed charges, taxes, adjustments, imports and export-credit rules can affect the remaining bill.

## 22. How much does solar cost?
There is no universal price. It depends on system size, equipment, batteries, installation, location and current market conditions.

## 23. Which panel is best?
Compare efficiency, power, warranty, temperature characteristics, price, availability and compatibility.

## 24. Which inverter should I choose?
Base the choice on peak load, PV size, MPPT limits, battery needs, phase, grid requirements, warranty and service.

## 25. How long do panels last?
They are designed for long-term operation; exact warranties and degradation guarantees vary by model.

## 26. What maintenance is required?
Inspect panels, cables, mounting, inverter status, protection and batteries where present.

## 27. What is a product warranty?
Coverage for qualifying product defects under the manufacturer's terms.

## 28. What is a performance warranty?
A manufacturer's specified guarantee of minimum output retention over time.

## 29. What information is needed for a solar recommendation?
City, monthly kWh, bill, roof area, loads, peak/critical load, backup hours, system type and battery requirement.

## 30. Can the chatbot design my final solar system?
It can provide an initial estimate, but final electrical, structural and interconnection design should be completed by qualified professionals.
